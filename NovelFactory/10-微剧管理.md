# 微剧管理

> 基础路径: `/api/novel-weiju`

## Banner 接口

### POST /banner/createBanner — 创建Banner (权限: ROLE_0, ROLE_1, ROLE_2)

**入参**: AppWeijuBanner对象 (JSON Body)  
**出参**: `Result<AppWeijuBanner>`

### GET /banner/getBannerByBannerId — 根据bannerId获取单个Banner

**入参** (Query): bannerId (String, 必填)  
**出参**: `Result<AppWeijuBanner>`

### GET /banner/getBannersByBannerId — 根据bannerId获取所有Banner

**入参** (Query): bannerId (String, 必填)  
**出参**: `Result<List<AppWeijuBanner>>`

### POST /banner/updateBanner — 更新Banner (权限: ROLE_0, ROLE_1, ROLE_2)

**入参**: AppWeijuBanner对象  
**出参**: `Result<AppWeijuBanner>`

### GET /banner/deleteBannerByBannerId — 根据bannerId删除Banner (权限: ROLE_0, ROLE_1, ROLE_2)

**入参** (Query): bannerId (String, 必填)  
**出参**: `Result<String>`

### GET /banner/deleteByAdId — 根据adId删除单个Banner (权限: ROLE_0, ROLE_1, ROLE_2)

**入参** (Query): adId (Integer, 必填)  
**出参**: `Result<String>`

## 通用接口

### GET /getAllAliases — 获取所有alias列表

**入参**: 无  
**出参**: `Result<Map>` 含 businessTypeAliases(List) 和 publicSwitchAliases(List)

### POST /batchEnableConfig — 批量启用配置 (权限: ROLE_0, ROLE_1, ROLE_2)

**入参**: `{ "businessTypeAlias": "string", "publicSwitchAlias": "string" }`  
**出参**: `Result<String>`
